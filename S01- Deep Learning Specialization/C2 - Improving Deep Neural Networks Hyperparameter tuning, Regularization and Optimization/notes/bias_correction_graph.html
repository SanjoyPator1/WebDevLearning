Bias Correction in Exponentially Weighted Averages

Vt = β × Vt-1 + (1-β) × θt
Vcorrected = Vt / (1 - βt)
⚠️ Andrew's Key Insight: "If you implement the formula as written, you won't actually get the green curve when Beta equals 0.98, you actually get the purple curve that starts off really low."
Uncorrected EWA (Purple Line)
Standard formula with V₀ = 0

The problem Andrew identified!
Bias Corrected EWA (Green Line)
Divides by (1 - β^t)
Removes initialization bias
Accurate estimates from day 1
Bias Correction Factor: (1 - βt) → As t increases, this approaches 1, so correction becomes minimal

Why it matters: In machine learning, we want good estimates from the beginning, especially for momentum in gradient descent or Adam optimizer.

🎯 Real-World Application:

This bias correction is crucial in:

  • Adam Optimizer: Both momentum and RMSprop terms use bias correction
  • Financial Analysis: Getting accurate moving averages from day 1
  • Signal Processing: Avoiding initialization artifacts
