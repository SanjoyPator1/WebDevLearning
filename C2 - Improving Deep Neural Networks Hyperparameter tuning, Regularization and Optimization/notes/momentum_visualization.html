🚀 Gradient Descent vs Momentum Optimization

The Problem & Solution:

Standard Gradient Descent: Creates zigzag oscillations on elliptical cost functions, wasting steps and slowing convergence.

Momentum: Uses exponentially weighted averages of gradients to smooth out oscillations and accelerate convergence.
